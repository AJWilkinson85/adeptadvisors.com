What you'll actually be able to do afterward.

Six things. Each one practiced on your own material during the session, not explained on a slide.

01

Know what the tool is

How a language model produces an answer, why it sounds confident when it's wrong, and what that means for how much you trust it with money and customers.

02

Ask in a way that works

Context, examples, and constraints. The difference between "write me an email" and a draft you'd actually send, and how to make it repeatable.

03

Run your inbox through it

Triage, summarize, draft replies, chase the ones who went quiet. The same standards you'd hold a new assistant to.

04

Quote and propose faster

Turn a phone call's worth of notes into a clean scope and price, in your voice, with your terms. Then do it again for the next one in four minutes.

05

Clean up the books

Categorize transactions, catch the ones that are wrong, and get a plain-English read on the month without waiting for the accountant.

06

Decide what not to automate

Where the risk is real: customer data, legal language, anything with a signature. A short list of rules your whole team can follow.

Questions people ask before booking.

Which AI tools do you teach?

Whichever fits your business. Most sessions end up on Claude or ChatGPT for the writing work, plus whatever your accounting and email already are. I'm not reselling software and I don't take referral fees.

Do we need to be technical?

No. If you can send an email and open a spreadsheet, you're ready. The technical part is my job; the session is about your judgment, not your keyboard skills.

What about our customer data?

We talk about this in the first hour, because it matters. You'll leave with a short written rule for what can go into an AI tool and what can't, and we set up the tools so those rules are easy to follow.

Remote or in person?

Both. In the Phoenix metro I'll come to you. Anywhere else, we run it over video with screen sharing, and it works nearly as well.

What is the Adept Library?

The members area at members.adeptadvisors.com. Twelve modules, from knowing the tool through the inbox, quotes, the books, hiring, scheduling, vendors, and what to do when a job goes wrong, written so your office can work through them without me in the room, with every prompt saved as a recipe and a worksheet at the end of each lesson. Access comes with a Working Session (90 days) or Advisor on Call (ongoing).

Can you just build it for us instead?

Yes, that's the 30-Day Build. But I'll still teach you how it works, because a workflow nobody on your team understands stops working the first time a tool updates.

Tell me about the business.

I'll tell you what a session would cover. A short email is enough: what you do, how many people, and the one task you'd most like to stop doing by hand.
